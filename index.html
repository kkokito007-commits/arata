Arata Nakatsuji

Career Business

Co-founder and CEO of SAKRA. Chairman of JINEN Inc.

Arata is a member of the founding family behind OMRON, which is headquartered in Kyoto, and the tenth generation of the house of Maruyama Ōkyo, the celebrated eighteenth-century Kyoto painter.

He spent five years in Silicon Valley. In San Francisco he founded AI ventures in music and healthcare and led a multinational team of 40. He also runs LYTA CAPITAL, the company that manages his own assets.

Business

SAKRA

SAKRA acquires Japan’s enduring small and mid-sized companies as their owners retire, modernizes them with AI and holds them for the long term. Many of these companies have no successor. SAKRA keeps them, and the people and customers who depend on them, in business. Arata is co-founder and CEO.
